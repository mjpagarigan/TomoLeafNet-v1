## Notes

- These sources are Philippines-local or Philippines-focused and are better suited for Filipino farmers than generic global articles.
- Several entries are research studies rather than how-to guides, so they work best paired with simpler extension content in the app feed.
- This markdown file is prepared for PDF conversion on download.
